# Quickstart: Prompt 3 – BMT Brand Identity & Premium UX

## Purpose

Use this guide to validate the premium BMT experience direction before implementation begins.

## Prerequisites

- Access to the BMT dashboard, client, and driver app designs or prototype surfaces
- A review environment that can show light and dark themes
- Arabic and Latin sample content
- Realistic route, stop, vehicle, and captain examples

## Validation Scenarios

### 1. Brand Consistency Review

1. Open representative dashboard, client, and driver surfaces side by side.
2. Check whether the same design language is visible in color, typography, spacing, iconography, and component hierarchy.
3. Confirm the product feels premium and clearly BMT-branded without borrowing a competitor’s signature look.

**Expected outcome**: All surfaces feel like one product family.

### 2. Map Experience Review

1. Open a route map and a live trip map.
2. Inspect whether routes, stops, and vehicles are immediately distinguishable.
3. Switch between light and dark themes.

**Expected outcome**: The map remains branded, readable, and operationally clear in both themes.

### 3. Reusable Component Review

1. Review captain cards, stop cards, route summary cards, buttons, chips, badges, sheets, and dialogs.
2. Confirm their sizing, spacing, states, and hierarchy feel consistent.
3. Trigger loading, empty, error, and success states.

**Expected outcome**: Shared components behave and feel like one design system.

### 4. Accessibility and Responsiveness Review

1. Review the same screens on phone, tablet, and desktop widths.
2. Check that long Arabic and Latin text remains readable.
3. Confirm that high-contrast states, focus states, and reduced-motion behavior remain usable.

**Expected outcome**: The experience remains legible, premium, and accessible across supported layouts.

## Notes

- This feature is a UX specification and should be validated visually before implementation planning proceeds.
- No external interface contract is required because the feature defines internal product experience rules.
